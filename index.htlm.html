Bed Making — Self-Study Guide
Select a bed type below to view the step-by-step guide and complete your self-assessment checklist.

Single Bed Making

Single — 90 × 190 cm
  1. Strip the bedRemove all used linen — pillowcase, top sheet, bottom sheet, and mattress protector. Place in the laundry trolley.
  2. Inspect the mattressCheck for stains, damage, or odour. Flip or rotate the mattress if required per property SOP.
  3. Place the mattress protectorLay the protector evenly over the mattress, ensuring all four corners are secured with fitted elastics.
  4. Lay the bottom sheetCentre the sheet lengthwise. Tuck in at the head end first, pull taut, and make a hospital (45°) corner at each foot corner.
  5. Add the top sheetPlace with the hem facing up at the head. Leave a 30 cm turndown at the top. Tuck in sides and make hospital corners at the foot.
  6. Add the blanket / duvetAlign the blanket 15 cm below the top of the bed. Fold the top sheet over the blanket edge for a clean turndown.
  7. Dress the pillowInsert the pillow into the case. Fluff and place centred at the head of the bed, open end facing away from the door.
  8. Final inspectionStand at the foot of the bed. Check symmetry, tautness of sheets, and that the bed skirt (if any) hangs evenly.
✅ Pro Tips
  • Always work from the same side of the bed to save time and steps.
  • Use your body weight (not just arms) to pull the sheet taut.
  • Keep corners crisp — 45° mitre is the industry standard.
⚠️ Common Mistakes
  • Uneven sheet overhang on left vs right side.
  • Pillow placed with the open end facing the door.
  • Blanket not aligned symmetrically.

Queen Bed Making

Queen — 150 × 200 cm
  1. Strip the bedRemove all linen including 2 pillowcases, top sheet, bottom sheet, and mattress protector.
  2. Inspect & rotate the mattressQueen beds are heavier — use two-person rotation where possible. Check for wear on both sides.
  3. Place the mattress protectorQueen protectors have deeper pockets. Ensure all four corners are fully stretched and fitted.
  4. Lay the bottom sheetStart from the head. The wider sheet requires you to walk around the bed more — keep it centred with equal overhang on both sides (approx 30–35 cm each side).
  5. Add the top sheetHem side up at the head. Create a 30 cm turndown. Tuck in both sides firmly and make hospital corners at the foot — both left and right.
  6. Add the blanket / duvetAlign 15 cm from the head. Fold the top sheet over the blanket. Ensure equal overhang on both sides of the bed.
  7. Dress 2 pillowsFluff both pillows. Place side by side at the head with open ends facing each other (or both away from door — follow property SOP).
  8. Final inspectionCheck from both the foot and the side. Ensure centre seam of the top sheet aligns with the centre of the bed.
✅ Pro Tips
  • Always start tucking from the head and work towards the foot — prevents re-work.
  • For a queen, the top sheet overhang should be symmetrical on left and right.
  • Two pillows should be level with each other — use the headboard as a reference line.
⚠️ Common Mistakes
  • Uneven side overhang — one side tucked too tight, the other too loose.
  • Pillows at different heights or angled instead of straight.
  • Mattress protector not fully covering corners — gaps expose the mattress.

King Bed Making

King — 180 × 200 cm
  1. Strip the bedRemove all linen. King beds may have 2–3 pillows (or king-size pillows). Remove all pillowcases and place in laundry.
  2. Inspect & rotate the mattressKing mattresses are very heavy — always use two people. Rotate head-to-foot or flip according to the rotation schedule.
  3. Place the mattress protectorKing protectors have the deepest pockets. Work your way around all four corners, stretching the elastic fully under the mattress.
  4. Lay the bottom sheetKing sheets require extra attention to centring. Overhang should be 35–40 cm on all sides. Tuck in head first, then walk to the foot and tuck firmly.
  5. Add the top sheetThe king top sheet is wider. Ensure it is perfectly centred using the centre seam. Create hospital corners at both foot corners. Tuck sides, leaving a small amount loose for guest comfort.
  6. Add the blanket / duvetKing duvets are heavier — shake and fluff before placing. Align 15 cm from the head. The duvet should hang equally on both sides and at the foot.
  7. Add the decorative runner (if applicable)Fold the decorative runner in thirds and place across the foot of the bed, aligned with the edge.
  8. Dress 2–3 pillowsUse king-size pillowcases if available. Stack or arrange pillows per property SOP. Ensure they are plumped and free of lumps.
  9. Final inspectionWalk around the entire bed. Check all four sides for symmetry, tautness, corner neatness, and pillow alignment.
✅ Pro Tips
  • For a king bed, always have a partner — it saves time and ensures symmetry.
  • Use the centre seam of the sheet as your alignment guide throughout.
  • Walk around all four sides before declaring the bed complete.
⚠️ Common Mistakes
  • Bottom sheet not centred — one side has significantly more overhang than the other.
  • Duvet not shaken/fluffed — lumpy appearance.
  • Pillows not aligned to a straight horizontal line.
  • Skipping the walk-around final inspection.
